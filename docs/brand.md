# NEXORA Brand

## Product name
NEXORA

## Positioning
The Operating Platform for Connected Businesses.

## Product descriptor
Network, billing and connectivity operations platform.

## Naming architecture
- NEXORA — master product brand
- NEXORA Billing — billing and revenue operations
- NEXORA Network — routers, hotspots, RADIUS and monitoring
- NEXORA Payments — payment collection and reconciliation
- NEXORA Connect — network connectivity and integrations
- NEXORA Analytics — reporting, intelligence and forecasting

## Brand rule
Do not use "YURIAN WiFi Billing" as the product-facing name in UI, documentation, metadata, marketing copy or product architecture. YURIAN may remain the parent/company identity where legally or organizationally required.
